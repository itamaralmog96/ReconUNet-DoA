ReconUNet Revision Results

Four learned front ends and six classical estimators evaluated on the corrected renderer, where multipath replicas are genuinely coherent with the direct path. Every model was retrained on the same 2 million scenes with the same schedule between 6 and 10 September 2026.

Headline

On the 12,000-scene paper test split, ReconUNet with a Root-MUSIC back end has the lowest pooled RMSE of any method and wins every source-count bucket. DA-MUSIC, the baseline the reviewers asked for, lands second on the pooled metric despite the worst median. SubViT is the sharpest on the median and the worst on the tail, including against the classical estimators.

  • ReconUNet leads by 1.4× over the next learned method and 2.1× over classical Root-MUSIC on the pooled metric, and is the only method whose error is flat across K on the scenario sweep.
  • Coherent multipath is now visibly the failure mode of classical subspace methods. In the Moderate scenario Root-MUSIC sits at 5.9° from −5 dB upward regardless of SNR, the rank-collapse signature Section II-B predicts. ReconUNet brings it to 1.8°.
  • Two honest limits. With one clean source and mild imperfections, classical Root-MUSIC edges every learned method above 0 dB. With one source plus six coherent replicas on eight elements, every learned method lands between 21° and 24° and none recovers.

What changed in the data

The July audit found that this repository's rewritten renderer produced replicas that were uncorrelated with the direct path (the submitted manuscript's results used the original generator and were never affected): sources were white at the sample rate, so a delay of one sample already produced an independent copy, and 80 % of delays saturated at a clip. The legacy generator band-limited every source to 10 % of Nyquist, giving a coherence time of about 20 samples. The renderer now does the same and applies the legacy fractional delays exactly through an FFT phase ramp. Rendering is lazy from the manifests, so no corpus was regenerated, but every observation changed and every checkpoint had to be retrained.

A second consequence matters for the method itself: the non-zero lags of the autocorrelation stack now carry the signal subspace while white noise decorrelates, which is the premise of the multi-lag input. Before the fix they carried nothing but noise.

Training runs

All runs used the paper schedule: Adam at 1e-4, weight decay 1e-5, batch 2048, up to 300 epochs, ReduceLROnPlateau 0.7/5, early stopping with patience 25, gradient clipping at 1.0. DA-MUSIC has a fixed-width head, so following its published protocol one instance was trained per source count on that count's 500k-scene subset.

Checkpoints were selected on validation loss. For ReconUNet and SubViT the validation RMSPE was still creeping down when the composite loss plateaued, and the three DA-MUSIC instances for K ≥ 2 reached the 300-epoch budget with their best epoch at the end. Both facts belong in the protocol paragraph.

Notes for the manuscript

  • Claim to make. ReconUNet is the only front end that is strong in every regime: best pooled RMSE on the test split and in Moderate and Crowded, never catastrophic, flat across K. Do not claim it wins everywhere.
  • Basic scenario. With a single clean source and mild imperfections, raw Root-MUSIC at 0.25° beats aided Root-MUSIC at 0.37°. Every learned method has an error floor near 0.3 to 0.6° above 0 dB, set by the imperfection prior it learned. Say so; it answers Reviewer 2's identifiability point directly.
  • Out-of-distribution scenario. Seven coherent arrivals on eight elements is a degrees-of-freedom limit. Classical breaks to 41°; all four learned methods sit between 21° and 24° with SubspaceNet marginally ahead of ReconUNet. Report it as a regime where no method recovers, and fold the high-SNR plateau into Limitations.
  • SubViT. The full-capacity 42.6 M ViT has the best median on the test split and the worst tail of any method, and it collapses under harsh imperfections in Moderate. It overfits the imperfection statistics of the training corpus. This is a publishable observation, not a footnote.
  • DA-MUSIC protocol. One model per K, K known, batched forward with a Hermitian PSD surrogate and the same robust eigendecomposition SubspaceNet uses. State each deviation from the vendored code; they are listed in the adapter's docstring.
  • Section II-B and the multipath text. The data now match the rank-1 derivation, so Section II-B stands. Replace the informal lag-structure argument with the measured autocorrelation profile and the correlation-versus-delay relation, which answers Reviewer 1's request for analysis.
  • Moderate scenario definition. The corpus here is 2 direct plus 1 replica as Section IV states; the submitted Table II caption said 3 + 1. Pick one and make the text agree.
  • Loss weights. All four composite-loss weights are 1.0 in the configuration that trained the model behind every number on this page.
